Questions about Vanto

What exactly does Vanto do?

It collects what you copy, lets you change the order, and pastes each item one by one. One small job, done properly.

What can I put in the queue?

Text, images, individual files, and groups of files. Everything stays visible in one compact menu-bar window.

Can I paste several text items at once?

Yes. Combine the text queue into one paste, in your chosen order. Separate items with a line break, space, comma, or your own separator.

Can I change the shortcuts and language?

Both global shortcuts can be changed in Settings. The app is available in English, Russian, Spanish, German, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Brazilian Portuguese.

Does my clipboard leave my Mac?

No. Vanto works locally, requires no account, and sends none of your copied content to the internet.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

macOS requires that permission so Vanto can listen for its global shortcuts and paste into the app you are using.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.

Is it a subscription?

No. One purchase gives you a lifetime license for up to three of your devices.

Can I download it again or get a refund?

You can download Vanto again from this website at any time. If Vanto isn’t right for you, you can ask for a refund within 30 days of purchase. See the refund policy.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Vanto checks for new versions, then downloads and installs them on its own. There is nothing to set up.
